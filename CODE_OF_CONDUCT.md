# Code of conduct

Verity adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
by reference, including its standards, scope, and enforcement guidelines.

Treat everyone with respect, welcome people with different backgrounds and
experience, and give constructive feedback about the work. Take responsibility
for mistakes and respect others' privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
sexual misconduct, personal attacks, and sharing private information without
consent are unacceptable.

This applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community
spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, Piotr Godlewski
(`@pgodlews`), using the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not post
private incident details in a public issue.

The maintainer reviews reports fairly and respects the reporter's privacy and
security. Enforcement follows the Covenant's guidelines and may include a
correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban, according to the impact
and pattern of the behavior. Project content or contributions that violate
these standards may be removed or rejected.
